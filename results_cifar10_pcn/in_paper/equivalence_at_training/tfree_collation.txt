WHAT THE GRID SUPPORTS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

1. THE CROSSING BUDGET IS RETIRED -- do not quote it. In THIS 512 draw the
   float32 crossing is at 8 for gamma_V 5e-5..1e-1 at every beta measured (3, 30,
   100), and at 9 for gamma_V=1e-5 at all three beta. But the sign AT T=7 flips
   with the draw: at gamma_V=1e-4, beta=30, same instrument, the 512 draw reads
   -0.6109 and the superseded 128-interpolant draw reads +0.4391. The crossing is
   therefore 8 in one draw and 7 in another at identical settings, and no crossing
   budget belongs in the paper. (Dropped from the main text and appendix
   2026-09-09.)

2. WHAT EVERY DRAW AGREES ON, and what the paper now says: the SHAPE. Strongly
   negative at T<=6, above 0.9 at T=8, reaching 0.99 by 10. What gamma changes is
   the plateau height, not the shape; gamma_V=1e-5 is the one setting that
   recovers late, at BOTH beta=3 and beta=100, so that is a property of gamma and
   not of the displacement size. Below 1e-5 (5e-6, 1e-6) the update NEVER turns
   positive, out to T_free=30.

3. "Reaches 0.99 by T_free=10": HOLDS IN FLOAT32 in every cell that reaches 0.99
   at all. The slowest are gamma_V=1e-4 at beta=3 and gamma_V=1e-2 at beta=3, both
   at exactly 10; every beta>=30 cell reaches it at 9.
   IT DOES NOT HOLD IN FLOAT64: every float64 cell that reaches 0.99 needs 12
   (T=10 reads 0.9894, just short). Do not write "by 10" without "in float32".

4. NO FLOAT64 CROSSING CLAIM IS MADE (dropped 2026-09-09). For the record the
   float64 rows here cross at 7 for gamma_V <= 3e-4 and at 8 for gamma_V >= 1e-3,
   but the low-gamma half is a knife edge -- the T=7 values are +0.0100, +0.0099,
   +0.0099, +0.0098, +0.0102, +0.0031, -0.0173 across gamma_V 1e-6..1e-3, all
   within 0.02 of zero. Only 1e-2 and 1e-1 cross at 8 for a real reason (-0.287,
   -0.520 at T=7).

5. THE f32-vs-f64 COMPARISON ON THIS AXIS IS CONFOUNDED BY BATCH GEOMETRY and is
   not made in the paper. float64 at B=128 does not fit, so every float64 row here
   is 32x16 against float32's 128x4 -- different draws. The tell: at T=8 float64
   reads 0.705 where float32 reads 0.96 at the same gamma and beta, and float64
   cannot be genuinely worse than float32 on the same samples. The float64 curve
   is a harder draw, not a slower convergence. Float64's role in §4.2 is the
   PLATEAU HEIGHT, a same-draw comparison across gamma (EXPLANATION §2, rows F).
